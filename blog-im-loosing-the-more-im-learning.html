i'm loosing, the more i'm learning - a poem

October 2026

Sounds of ghazal playing in the background, I take another slurp of chai. I can't help but wonder why the world is in such a shitty state.

This has been happening for the past few weeks. (Part of) Addison Crump's essay does a good job of explaining my state of mind.1

The military's suppression in Pakistan. Student protests in India. New immigration rules in the US. Big tech making yet another mindless move to feed its capitalist hunger. The rise of the right wing in Germany. Protests in France, Spain, Kazakhstan, the UK. Saudi Arabia's military campaign in Yemen. The genocide in Palestine.

Then one day I wanted to write it all down. It came out as a nazm (or poem).

Roman UrduEnglish

Mein haar raha hoonJaisay jaisay jaan raha hoon

I'm loosing,The more I'm learning

Iqtasadiat ki iss jung meinGadagar ki siskiaan sun raha hoon

In this war of personal interests,I'm hearing the sobs of the helpless

Zulm o barbariat kay natak koZanjeeroon mein jakra hua seh raha hoon

This theater of cruelty,I'm enduring in chains

Awaaz uthanay waloon ka garaybaanAskariyat kay haathoon chaak hota dekh raha hoon

The collars of those speaking up,I'm watching being torn by military hands

Haqeeqat jaan-nay kay bawajoodQadam say qadam milanay say kaamp raha hoon

Despite knowing the truth,I'm trembling to walk beside them

Apni hee cheekhoon ko sun karAndar hee andar damm tor raha hoon

Listening to my own screams,I'm suffocating inside

Mein haar raha hoon aurAhista ahista emaan kho raha hoon

I'm loosing and,Slowly losing faith
